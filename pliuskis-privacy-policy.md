# Privacy Policy for Pliuškis

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pliuškis ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application for Android and iOS. Pliuškis lets you find your Pliuškis plant devices, read their measurements and water your plants.

## Information We Collect

We collect only what the app needs to sign you in and to connect you to your devices.

### Account Information
You sign in with your Google account or your Apple ID. We do not offer sign-up with a user name and password. When you sign in, we receive and store:
- Your email address (if you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we receive Apple's relay address instead)
- The unique user ID that Google or Apple gives us for your account
- An identity ID that links your account to the temporary credentials the app uses to reach your devices

We never receive your Google or Apple password.

### Device Data
When you use the app, it sends and receives messages about your plant devices:
- Device information and availability (for example, whether a device is online)
- Measurements such as soil moisture and water tank level
- Commands you send, such as watering a plant

These messages pass through our device messaging service so the app can show them to you. We do not keep a history of your measurements or commands.

### Technical Logs
Our cloud provider may record short technical logs when a connection fails (for example, the connection ID of the app and the reason for the failure). We use them only to find and fix problems.

## How We Use Information

We use this information only to:
- Sign you in and keep your account secure
- Approve your account for access to devices (we approve each account by hand)
- Show your devices' measurements and carry out your commands
- Fix problems with the app and the service

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising or build a profile of you.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

### Internet and Network State
Used to sign you in and to connect to your plant devices.

### Local Network (iOS)
Used to find your plant devices and talk to them on your local network.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses these services, each with its own privacy policy:
- **Google Sign-In** and **Sign in with Apple** to sign you in
- **Amazon Web Services** (Amazon Cognito and AWS IoT) to manage accounts and carry device messages. Your data is stored in the AWS region eu-north-1 (Stockholm, Sweden), within the European Union.

The app does not use analytics tools, advertising networks or tracking technologies.

## Data Retention and Deleting Your Account

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app, or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes your account information from our service.

## Data Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can reach devices only after we approve it, and each app connection is limited to its own account.

## Your Rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. We process your data because it is needed to provide the app to you. To use any of these rights, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Lithuania, this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
